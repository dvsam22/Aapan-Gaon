Child Safety Standards & CSAE Prevention Policy

Effective Date: July 2026

🚫 Zero Tolerance Policy

Apna Gaon has a strict Zero Tolerance Policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE), and any form of child endangerment. We are fully committed to protecting children across all digital interactions and services.

🛡️ 1. Commitment to Child Protection

At Apna Gaon, our mission is to connect local rural communities safely. We enforce strict guidelines to ensure children are protected from sexual exploitation, abuse, human trafficking, or inappropriate contact.

We strictly comply with national and international child safety laws, including the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act and global Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) / Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE) compliance frameworks.

📜 2. Safety Standards & Prohibited Conduct

Users, service providers, and vendors on Apna Gaon are strictly prohibited from:

  • Creating, uploading, sharing, or transmitting any Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE) content.
  • Engaging in grooming, solicitation, or inappropriate communication directed towards minors.
  • Depicting minors in any sexually suggestive, harmful, or exploitative context.
  • Promoting or facilitating child labor, trafficking, or child abuse in any form.

🚨 3. In-App Reporting & Moderation

We provide robust mechanisms to report and moderate any suspicious activity or content immediately:

  • In-App Reporting: Users can flag any listing, user profile, or service request directly within the app using the 1-click Report button.
  • Proactive Review: Content flagged for child safety concerns is prioritized for immediate administrative review within 1 hour.
  • Account Termination: Any user or service provider found violating child safety policies will face permanent account suspension and device ban without prior warning.

⚖️ 4. Law Enforcement Cooperation

Upon detecting any CSAM/CSAE material or illegal child endangerment activities, Apna Gaon takes immediate law enforcement action:

  • We report CSAM content directly to relevant national and international cybercrime reporting authorities, including the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
  • We fully preserve forensic evidence and cooperate with law enforcement investigations to bring offenders to justice.
