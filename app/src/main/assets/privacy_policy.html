Device Permissions We Use

To keep our application lightweight and secure, we only request the essential permissions required to deliver local services:

Allows you to tap "Call Now" on any vendor or doctor card to open your phone's native dialer directly. We never record or store your calls.
🔔 Push Notifications Permission
Used strictly to send you important village notices, emergency updates, and Mandi market crop rates.

If you register as a vendor (Tractor Owner, Decorator, Doctor, etc.), your Name, Mobile Number, and Village are listed so nearby users can call you.
