PRIVACY POLICY (गोपनीयता नीति) — APNA GAON

Last Updated: July 2026

Welcome to Apna Gaon ("we," "our," or "us"). We are highly committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle information in our mobile application.

A. Information We Collect & Device Permissions

To keep our application lightweight and secure, we only request the absolute minimum permissions required to run the service:

  • Phone Dialer Permission (Direct Call): The app requests permission to interact with your device's native phone dialer. When you click the "Call Now" button on any worker, doctor, or vendor card, it automatically opens your phone's dialer with their number pre-filled. We do not monitor, record, or store your personal calls.
  • Notification Permission: The app requests permission to send push notifications to your device. This is used strictly to send you hyperlocal news updates, seasonal mandi rates, emergency alerts, or festival announcements.
  • Profile Data (For Service Providers/Vendors): If you register as a vendor (Tractor Owner, Decorator, Caterer, Doctor, etc.), we collect your Name, Mobile Number, Village/Town Location, and Service Category to display publicly so users can find and call you.

B. How We Use Your Information

  • To display a digital directory of local services so village users can easily find providers.
  • To enable seamless direct calling between users and providers via the native dialer.
  • To send important local updates and notices through push notifications.

C. Data Sharing and Third Parties

  • Public Visibility for Vendors: If you choose to list your business or service on Apna Gaon, your business name, mobile number, and location will be visible to all app users to facilitate direct communication.
  • Data Protection: We do not sell, trade, or share user data with any third-party marketing companies or advertising networks.

D. Data Security

We secure your registered information using industry-standard security practices. However, since vendor mobile numbers are displayed publicly to facilitate direct calls, we cannot control how individuals use those numbers outside the application.
