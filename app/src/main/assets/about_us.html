📱 About Us — APNA GAON

Apna Gaon is a digital directory and local services platform specifically designed to empower rural areas and small towns. Our primary mission is to bring the power of Digital India directly to your village by making local services easily accessible.

🌟 Our Mission

Finding a tractor for rent, hiring a wedding caterer, booking tent house services, or locating skilled laborers and doctors on short notice can be challenging in rural areas.

Apna Gaon bridges this gap by directly connecting villagers with trusted local service providers. With just a single click, users can quickly find the required service and connect instantly through direct phone calls.

🛠️ Key Features We Provide

  • Hyperlocal Services: Find everything you need in one place—from tractor rentals, catering services, tent houses, and doctors to skilled laborers and many other local businesses.
  • One-Click Calling: Simply tap the "Call Now" button to open your phone's dialer and speak directly with the service provider.
  • Local Updates: Stay informed with important village notices, daily Mandi market rates, and emergency contact information.
